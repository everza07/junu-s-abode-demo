Privacy Policy

  
Last updated: 9 November 2026

  
Junu's Abode ("we", "us") is a homestay at West Gotanagar, Gopalthan, Ward No. 2, House No. 6, C/o Homoeoghar, Guwahati, Assam 781011. This policy explains what personal information we collect from guests and website visitors, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

  
What we collect

  
    
    
  • Enquiry details. Your name, travel dates, number of guests, room preference and any note you write, when you message us on WhatsApp or by phone.
    • 
    
  • Contact details. Your phone number, which WhatsApp shares with us when you message us.
    • 
    
  • Check-in details. A valid photo ID and the names of guests, as Indian rules for accommodation providers require. Foreign nationals may also be asked for passport and visa details.
    • 
    
  • Website data. This website does not use accounts, trackers or advertising. It stores only your theme choice and your cookie notice choice in your own browser.
    • 
  

  
How the enquiry form works

  
The form on this site prepares a message on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers. The message is sent only if you choose to open WhatsApp and press send, and WhatsApp's own privacy terms then apply.

  
Why we use your information

  
    
    
  • To reply to your enquiry and confirm availability and rates.
    • 
    
  • To manage your booking and your stay, including early check-in requests.
    • 
    
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping guest records and responding to lawful requests from authorities.
    • 
    
  • To keep our guests and premises safe.
    • 
  

  
Sharing

  
We do not sell your information. We share it only with staff who need it to host you, with service providers such as the messaging app you chose to contact us on, and with authorities when the law requires it.

  
How long we keep it

  
We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to handle your booking. We keep guest registration records for the period the law requires, then delete them.

  
Your rights

  
You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where the law allows. You can also withdraw consent for messages at any time. Contact us using the details below.

  
Children

  
We do not knowingly collect information about children except as part of a family booking made by a parent or guardian.

  
Security

  
We keep guest records with the host and staff only, and take reasonable steps to protect them. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

  
Changes

  
We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
